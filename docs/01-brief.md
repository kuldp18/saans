# Product brief

## Problem

A red AQI badge tells people conditions are bad. It rarely tells a family how to handle a fixed school run, when to move flexible errands, or what to do at home on a tight budget. Community organizers also need a clear message they can share without collecting participant information.

## Product

Saans converts a city forecast and a short routine form into an ordered action plan. Every action answers what to change, why it helps and what the estimate actually measures. Focus on urban India, beginning with Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Lucknow, Ahmedabad and Kolkata. City selection is explicit and static for the MVP. No GPS or inferred location.

| Adult user | Need | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household planner | Keep a fixed trip and move a flexible activity | The result keeps fixed commitments and shows a feasible flexible option or says none qualifies. |
| Budget-conscious resident | Reduce indoor particle sources using available resources | At least one relevant no-purchase action is easy to find even without a purifier. |
| Commuter | Compare a lower-emission choice without assuming it improves personal exposure | Show travel emissions and outdoor air as distinct effects, with a plain description of tradeoffs. |
| Building or community coordinator | Share an understandable short notice | Copy or download a source-stamped text notice, with no household inputs or participant data. |

## Main journeys

- **Household:** City → Household → time flexibility, budget and transport options → plan → compare one action.
- **Community:** City → Community → select an outdoor activity and possible indoor venue → plan → copy a notice with one protective and one lower-emission action.
- **Fast demo:** Load a synthetic Delhi smog scenario with one click. The routine form is editable and results change predictably.

## Differentiators

- A fixed-versus-flexible routine preserves practical constraints.
- Every card separates the likely benefit from the limits of the evidence. A cleaner forecast window, an indoor particle reduction mechanism and avoided car distance are three different outcomes.
- GenAI prioritizes and sequences contextually valid choices. It cannot invent guidance, measurements or rules.

## Scope

Responsive English web MVP with keyboard support. Support city-level modeled AQ data, sample scenarios, a reusable action catalog, a constrained Gemini planner and a clear no-key path. Exclude medical chat, routing/maps, sensor integrations, personal profiles, automatic city forecasts for unsupported locations, background alerts and public posting. Treat translation as an extension after core accessibility checks.
