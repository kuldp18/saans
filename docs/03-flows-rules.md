# Domain rules and impact

## Canonical air data

Return cityId, timeZone, sourceId, mode (live-model, stale-model, demo, unavailable), fetchedAt, and ordered hourly {validAt, pm25UgM3, usAqi}. Include source model time only if supplied. Parse upstream UTC timestamps explicitly, present city-local time and keep validAt distinct from fetchedAt. Define displayed current slot as the hourly bin containing the requested instant in UTC. Treat missing, non-finite, negative and nonconforming values as unavailable, never zero-fill. Require equal array lengths, documented units and unique valid timestamps. Pair the official CPCB link with a statement that its readings and scale are separate. Do not infer CPCB categories from modeled US AQI.

Use these US AQI bands after applying documented integer normalization: 0–50 Good, 51–100 Moderate, 101–150 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 151–200 Unhealthy, 201–300 Very unhealthy, 301+ Hazardous. Preserve null. If the provider contract caps values, validate against that cap. Do not infer US AQI from a simple average of hourly PM2.5. Sample data uses a fixed anchor, explicitly dated and marked synthetic.

## Eligibility before ranking

1. Normalize household or community inputs and clamp the search to the next 12 local hours after the common anchor, within available data. Only compare same-duration windows among feasible times. Half-open intervals [start,end) prevent double-counting. For the MVP allow half-hour start increments and 30–120 minute durations; use overlapping minutes as weights. A window with any missing or stale PM2.5 bin is ineligible. Both original and alternative need fresh, relevant data for a numeric comparison.
2. Exclude alternatives that miss time bounds, depend on unavailable equipment or transport, violate budget, or move a fixed commitment. If no cleaner feasible window exists, keep the commitment and offer an indoor option where feasible. No routine policy should call forecast air “safe.”
3. For optional strenuous outdoor activities, suppress recommended outdoor windows beginning at modeled US AQI 101 for a locally processed cautious preference and 151 for the default, using the highest AQI in the window. These are conservative product gates informed by AirNow activity guidance, not local mandates. Missing AQI also blocks recommendation of optional outdoor exertion. At 201 and above, lead with an official advisory link and prioritize indoor alternatives for optional outdoor activity. A fixed essential trip receives a practical lower-exposure or lower-emission alternative only when feasible, with local advisories visible. Avoid suggesting cycling or walking as a default replacement in harmful conditions.
4. Use a curated action catalog with stable IDs, source IDs, eligibility predicate, cost class, type, authored title, rationale, mechanism and caveat. Seed no-purchase actions such as skipping incense or candles during an episode and coordinating one combined errand. Offer particle filtration only with suitable equipment. Ventilation advice must be conditional on outdoor and indoor conditions and must not override safe ventilation, heat or combustion safety. Avoid ozone generators and blanket advice to seal a room. Keep government public guidance visible without personalized medical advice.
5. Provide at least one routine, one indoor and one travel action when eligible. Deterministic ties use earliest window then stable action ID. A condition that disqualifies one action cannot be overridden by ranking. Community share is derived from the approved plan, plus source, data mode and local valid time. Suppress time-specific language when live data is stale.

## Direct-impact examples and formulas

| Outcome | Calculation and display | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flexible time comparison | Duration-weighted outdoor modeled PM2.5: sum(value × overlapping minutes) ÷ duration. Delta = baseline − alternative. Percent = delta ÷ baseline × 100 when baseline > 0. Calculate with full precision, round once for display. | Same duration, grid and snapshot. An outdoor concentration comparison, not personal dose, measured indoor air, health risk or city change. If no positive difference, show no improvement. |
| Avoided car travel | Subtract proposed private-car vehicle-km from baseline private-car vehicle-km, both whole journeys. Only claim avoided distance for a real replacement or consolidation. For contributed vehicles count each vehicle once. | Show vehicle-km as a direct activity measure, no inferred CO₂/PM/NOₓ or claim of zero lifecycle emissions. Show why avoiding an engine trip can reduce tailpipe pollution. |
| Indoor sources | Show mechanism, e.g. “Skipping incense avoids adding smoke particles indoors” or “A suitable particle filter can capture particles in this room.” | No percentage, indoor reading or outcome guarantee without measurements. A filter does not remove all pollutants. |

## Golden scenario

Synthetic Delhi fixture at a versioned anchor during a winter date. Anchor local 07:00. Set 08:00 and 09:00 PM2.5 bins to 120 and 100; 13:00 and 14:00 to 60 and 40. For a non-strenuous flexible errand, 08:30–09:30 averages 110 and 13:30–14:30 averages 50 µg/m³. Display 60 µg/m³ lower (55%, rounded). Mark the morning as modeled unhealthy or worse in the fixture, and set afternoon band consistently with the independently seeded synthetic AQI test values. An independently editable two-errand example uses 16 private-car km versus 10 km and displays 6 vehicle-km avoided. Never use this synthetic fixture to imply an actual Delhi air reading.
